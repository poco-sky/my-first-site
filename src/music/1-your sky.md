[Verse 1]
When city lights all fade away,
Our dream, just ours, begins to sway.
You whisper, "Don't forget my dear,"
Those words inside, I hold them near.

[Pre-Chorus]
Beneath the pale and gentle moon,
We reach for stars that fade too soon.

[Chorus]
If your heart is the endless sky,
Then a shining star I want to fly,
To light the paths you cannot see,
I'll be right there, eternally.

[Verse 2]
Though mysteries start to unfold,
Your presence is more precious than gold.
You smile and say, "I won't forget,"
A memory, sparkling, never set.

[Pre-Chorus]
The city's hum begins to fade,
This precious moment, softly swayed.

[Chorus]
If your heart is the endless sky,
Then a whispering wind I want to fly,
When you feel lost, I'll guide your way,
I'll be right here, through every day.

[Bridge]
These feelings that I can't express,
Melt into night, a starry caress.
With every wish, three times I'll call,
Your name, my love, embracing all.

[Chorus]
If your heart is the endless sky,
Then a shining star I want to fly,
To light the future, wild and free,
I'll be right here, for you and me.

[Outro]
The night wind weaves a silent tune,
Your voice still echoes, by the moon.
