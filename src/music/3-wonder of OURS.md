A fleeting shadow in a silent space,

The world around begins to flee from me.

I wake up with this bittersweet enthrall.

A gentle sound to mask my soul's complaint.
I truly wish you’d see me, it is true,

[Pre-Chorus]
A glimpse of what sweet Heaven must have been.
By this uncertain yearning I am driven,

[Chorus]
I shoot an arrow in the memory haze,
Hoping that you’ll finally meet my gaze.
I pray that in your eyes, one distant day,
The only image staring back will be my way.

In nights embraced by perfect quiet air,
I still recall the profile that you wear.
My worries turn to hope that is so deep,
A lovely comfort that my soul can keep.
