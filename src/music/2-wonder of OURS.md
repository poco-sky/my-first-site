[Verse 1]
It's not your looks or your physique I'm facing,
But the genuine you that my heart is chasing.
A fleeting shadow in a silent space,
Invading the cracks in my mind's resting place.

[Pre-Chorus]
Each time I sketch the you I cannot see,
The world around begins to flee from me.
Like freezing when the summer breezes call,
I wake up with this bittersweet enthrall.

[Chorus]
I chime a bell in memories obscured and faint,
A gentle sound to mask my soul's complaint.
I truly wish you’d see me, it is true,
But think this distance is the best for you.

[Verse 2]
I said, "It's not your looks or your physique,"
Yet every little part of you is sweet.
From the first moment you came into sight,
The world I see was painted with new light.

[Pre-Chorus]
Within your eyes that shine so bright and keen,
A glimpse of what sweet Heaven must have been.
By this uncertain yearning I am driven,
And keep on searching for the love you've given.

[Chorus]
I shoot an arrow in the memory haze,
Hoping that you’ll finally meet my gaze.
I pray that in your eyes, one distant day,
The only image staring back will be my way.

[Bridge]
In nights embraced by perfect quiet air,
I still recall the profile that you wear.
My worries turn to hope that is so deep,
A lovely comfort that my soul can keep.
