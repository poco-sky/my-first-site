[Verse 1]
When the Morning Glory blooms so bright,
You awaken to the light.
The summer breeze, a gentle touch,
Calls out my name, I want you so much.

Beyond the curtain, pure and white,
I see your small silhouette in the light.
I just reach out my hand to feel your warmth,
Wishing to touch you, breaking through the storm.

[Chorus 1]
You are too dazzling for me to deeply ponder,
It feels like the sky, so blue, begins to taunt and wander.
I could only stare at you,
My heart just had to see it through.

[Verse 2]
When the Morning Glory starts to fade,
I know that I can finally be made
To go and meet you, but a winter gust,
Takes you away without any trust.

I stand beside you, just a little bit taller,
A mundane future, nothing smaller.
I only wish we could be side by side,
Just the two of us, nowhere to hide.

[Chorus 2]
The sky is too blue for me to feel this ache,
The bright sun seems to rush and gently shake.
The words I crumpled in my hand,
Just said "I love you," do you understand?

[Pre-Chorus]
The life of scattered petals, one by one,
I know they’ll be reborn when this season is done.
Every time the seasons come full circle,
I wonder if my heart will truly miracle.

[Final Chorus]
You were too wonderful for me to just forget,
But this wide sky will keep us connected yet.
My longing heart will take its flight,
Riding on the breeze, far out of sight.
